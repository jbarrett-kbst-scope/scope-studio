Three Pathway Pathways

Structured frameworks guiding student STEM research, engineering design, and enterprise creation.

Challenge Track

Designed for rigorous scientific research, manuscript publication, and competitive STEM entry.

  • Key Deliverable: Independent research paper, IRB/IACUC regulatory documentation
  • Target Platforms: Regeneron STS, IJHSR, JEI, NIH Challenge
Scholarly Research

Founder Track

Focuses on hardware startup creation, product design, and commercialization strategies.

  • Key Deliverable: Engineering documentation & functional physical prototype
  • Target Platform: Kickstarter Crowdfunding Campaign
Entrepreneurship

Client Track

Tailored for real-world client management, custom engineering solutions, and targeted pitches.

  • Key Deliverable: Engineering documentation, functional prototype & pitch deck
  • Target Platform: Live client presentation & functional demo

Featured Fall 2026 Projects

Current active proposals undergoing design, experimentation, and build phases.

Client Track Client: Dr. Barrett

Post-Suture Wound Healing Device

Developing dynamic daytime support bands, a light nocturnal alignment splint, and an advanced liquid bandage formula to maintain tension and support skin healing on high-movement joint areas like hands.

Biomedical Engineering Wound Care
Founder Track Crowdfunding Target

Outbreak Education Board Game

An engaging, rule-accessible board game tailored for elementary and middle school students to combat infectious disease misinformation and improve public health literacy.

Game Design Public Health
Challenge Track Genomics / Survey

Genetic Screening in Vietnamese Populations

Evaluating carrier frequencies for autosomal recessive disorders (Alpha Thalassemia, PKU) across Vietnamese ethnic subgroups, assessing testing barriers in young adults (18–25), and mapping low-cost prenatal screening methods.

Genomics IRB Approved
Challenge Track Computational Biology

Computational Colorectal Cancer Vulnerability Mapping

Utilizing a modified DAISY pipeline combining TCGA patient tumor data and DepMap CRISPR knockout screens to identify synthetic lethal targets in drug-resistant KRAS and BRAF mutant colorectal cancer lines.

Bioinformatics Oncology
